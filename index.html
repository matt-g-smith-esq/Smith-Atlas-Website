We're in the business of supporting yours.

All Smith-Atlas products and services are built on our company values to bring your organization our best work.

Intelligence Augmentation

We don't believe in offloading intellect. We believe in offloading workflow processes that AI tech can automate to benefit the human experience.

Humanity

People come first. We're not in the business of finding ways to eliminate jobs. Our focus is on augmenting work capacity with AI capabilities to place humanity at the helm: as overseers of AI automation and vanguards of new ways to advance missions.

AI Literacy

As great as innovation is, AI literacy opens doors. We attest to the importance of intellectual capital as fundamental in dismantling barriers, including resistance, to AI technology. (It's the classic "knowledge is power" mantra in the AI era.)

Social Responsibility

We believe the integrity of a business has to include caring about its impact on what's around us and what matters to the people around us. It's why we're eco-aware and design AI tools that minimize token usage.

Access

AI tools should be affordable, no matter the organization's size and budget. Pricetags shouldn't gatekeep. It's everyone's right to access impressive AI tools that can lighten workloads and transform operations.

Clarity

Shared understanding is important. As active listeners to the problems you're solving for, we value the transparency and honesty that belongs in each conversation. As your mapmakers, we'll bring this to the table every time.
